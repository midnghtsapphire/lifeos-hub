# Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence

LifeOS Hub helps people run life with confidence using one unified intelligence layer.

## Voice and tone

- Clear
- Calm
- Practical
- Empowering

## Messaging pillars

1. **Unified visibility:** one place for life operations.
2. **Actionable intelligence:** insights that lead to concrete next steps.
3. **Trust and control:** secure, user-owned data experience.

## Visual direction

- Dark cinematic UI baseline with gradient primary/accent treatment
- High contrast typography for dashboard readability
- Minimal, data-forward card composition

## Core tagline options

- “Your Life. Optimized.”
- “One Hub for Health, Wealth, and Protection.”
